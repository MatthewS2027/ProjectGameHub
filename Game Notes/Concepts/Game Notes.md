### Core Vision

This game will have many levels that involve killing waves of enemies / bosses and/or completing complex mechanics. The player will unlock abilities and new weapons while progressing the game. This will have an RNG system where different loot rarities will depend based on level difficulty or other challenges, and the actual specific piece of gear will be random among that rarity. Progression will include unlocking new levels, better weapons and abilities, and beating increasingly difficult levels. I want the game to focus less on content quantity and more on a short progression system with a decent end game experience. End game experience may be grinding very difficult levels to unlock rare weapons.

## Style

This game is completely Top-Down and 2D. I would like the theme to be Sci-Fi and have sort of a ghost / undead type of feel. A good reference is the Taken from the Destiny series.
