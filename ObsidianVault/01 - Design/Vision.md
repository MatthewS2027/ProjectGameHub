# 🌟 Vision

> Core concept, tone, and inspirations for the game.

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

_What is this game, in one sentence?_

> A top-down action game where the player runs through procedurally-influenced dungeons, fights waves of enemies, collects loot, and grows stronger with each run.

---

## 🎨 Tone & Feel

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Mood | Dark, tense, rewarding |
| Pacing | Fast and punchy — combat should feel impactful |
| Aesthetic | Gritty sci-fi / dungeon hybrid |
| Player Fantasy | Becoming an unstoppable force through skill and gear |

---

## 💡 Inspirations

- **Destiny** — Loot loop, gunfeel, moment-to-moment combat
- **Enter the Gungeon** — Room-based dungeon runs, risk/reward
- **Hades** — Narrative integration with roguelite runs
- **Diablo** — Loot tiers, enemy density, build variety

See also: [[Inspirations]]

---

## 🗝 Pillars

1. **Combat is king** — Every encounter should feel satisfying
2. **Loot drives motivation** — Rewards must feel meaningful
3. **Runs are short and replayable** — Players should want "one more run"
4. **Progression feels real** — The player must feel stronger over time
