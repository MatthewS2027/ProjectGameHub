# 📈 Progression

> Player growth, difficulty curve, and long-term engagement systems.

---

## 🧬 Player Growth

### Per-Run Progression
- Loot collected during a run upgrades the player's loadout
- Passive upgrades or perks chosen between levels
- All per-run progress is reset on death (roguelite)

### Meta Progression (Persistent)
- Currency or unlockables that persist between runs
- Unlocking new starting weapons, characters, or abilities
- Story/lore unlocks as runs are completed

| Source | Type | Notes |
