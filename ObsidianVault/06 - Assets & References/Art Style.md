# 🎨 Art Style

> Visual direction, color palette references, and aesthetic guidelines.

---

## 🖼 Overall Direction

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Genre | Top-down action |
| Art Style | Stylized pixel art / low-poly 2D |
| Mood | Dark, gritty, high-contrast |
| Time of Day | Primarily interior/underground — artificial lighting |
| Color Saturation | Muted base with vivid accent colors (loot, effects) |

---

## 💡 Lighting Guidelines

- Use rim lighting to separate characters from backgrounds
- Enemy projectiles: bright warm colors (orange, red)
- Player projectiles: bright cool colors (blue, white)
- Boss arenas: dramatic single-source lighting with colored accents
